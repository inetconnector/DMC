Local AI - DMC brings capable AI conversations directly to your Android device. Run compatible GGUF language models locally, keep long conversations available, and receive responses as they are generated.

LOCAL-FIRST AI
Your model, conversations and attachments stay on your device during local AI inference. No cloud account is required for chatting. Network access occurs for downloads or external connections you initiate and for technical diagnostics and usage data from the included Google ML Kit features. ML Kit processes image and text content on the device.

LONG, RELIABLE CONTEXT
Deterministic Multiresolution Context (DMC) preserves important parts of long histories when the physical model cache fills. It combines a stable global prefix, recent conversation detail and deterministic replay levels without recursively summarizing your original text. The available context size depends on the selected model and device resources.

MULTIMODAL INPUT
Attach images, take a photo, add text documents or PDFs, and use voice input. Image and audio capabilities depend on the selected model. Responses stream into the conversation while the model is working.

THREE-DAY TRIAL, ONE-TIME UNLOCK
Try the app free for three days. Afterwards, a single purchase permanently unlocks full access. There is no subscription. Google Play displays the price that applies in your country before purchase. After your explicit confirmation, only a pseudonymous installation identifier, app version and language are transmitted to provide the one-time trial, never chats, files, models or prompts.

BUILT FOR CONTROL
- Local GGUF model management
- Resumable model downloads
- Persistent chats with isolated conversation context
- Import and export for conversations, settings and models
- Camera, image, audio, text and PDF attachments
- Configurable generation and sampling settings
- CPU-focused Android inference

Model compatibility, speed and maximum context vary by model, quantization, available memory and device. Local AI - DMC is a general-purpose AI tool and contains no medical knowledge, diagnosis or treatment functionality.
